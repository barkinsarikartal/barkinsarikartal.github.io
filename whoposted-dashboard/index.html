Whoposted Dashboard

Whoposted Dashboard is a private tool that Barkın Sarıkartal uses to follow the business numbers of his own app, Whoposted: players, games, installs and ad revenue. It collects these numbers from his own accounts and shows them on a private web page, so he can follow them from his phone.

It is not offered to anyone else. Only the owner connects his accounts to it, and only people he invites can view it.

Whoposted Dashboard, Barkın Sarıkartal'ın kendi uygulaması Whoposted'ın iş verilerini (oyuncular, oyunlar, indirmeler, reklam geliri) kendi hesaplarından toplayıp özel bir sayfada gösteren kişisel bir araçtır. Başka kimseye sunulmaz.
