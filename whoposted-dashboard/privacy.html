Whoposted Dashboard privacy policy

Last updated: 9 November 2026

Who uses Whoposted Dashboard

Whoposted Dashboard is a private tool run by its owner, Barkın Sarıkartal, for his own app, Whoposted. Only the owner connects his Google account to it. A few people he invites can view the dashboard page; they never sign in to Google through it. It is not offered to other people.

What it accesses in Google

Whoposted Dashboard asks for one Google permission: admob.readonly. With it, the tool can only read the owner's own AdMob reports (estimated earnings, ad requests, impressions and similar totals, by day, app, platform, country and ad format). It cannot change anything in AdMob, and it cannot see the owner's email, contacts, files or any other Google data.

Separately, a read-only service account that the owner created reads his own Google Play Console statistics reports (installs and uninstalls by day and country).

What it does with that access

The tool saves these report totals and shows them as numbers and charts on the owner's private dashboard. It does not share, sell or transfer any data to anyone, and it does not use the data for advertising.

Whoposted Dashboard's use and transfer of information received from Google APIs adheres to the Google API Services User Data Policy, including the Limited Use requirements.

Where data is kept

The report totals are kept in the owner's own database on his Cloudflare account. The Google sign-in token is stored only as an encrypted secret on the owner's own Cloudflare account and on his own computer. The data contains no personal information about Whoposted's players; AdMob and Google Play reports are totals only.

Removing access

The owner can remove the tool's access at any time from his Google account settings (Security, Third-party apps with account access), and can delete the stored totals from his own database.
