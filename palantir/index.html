Palantir

Palantir is a private tool that Barkın Sarıkartal uses for his own social media work. It collects the statistics of his own Instagram, TikTok and YouTube accounts and writes a readable copy of them into his own Google Drive, so he can follow them from his phone.

It is not offered to anyone else, and nobody else can sign in to it.

Palantir, Barkın Sarıkartal'ın kendi sosyal medya hesaplarının istatistiklerini kendi Google Drive'ına kopyalamak için kullandığı kişisel bir araçtır. Başka kimseye sunulmaz.
