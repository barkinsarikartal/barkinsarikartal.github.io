Palantir privacy policy

Last updated: 22 October 2026

Who uses Palantir

Palantir is a private tool run by its owner, Barkın Sarıkartal, for his own social media accounts. Only the owner signs in to it. It is not offered to other people.

What it accesses in Google

Palantir asks for one Google permission: drive.file. With it, Palantir can only see and change the Google Drive files that it created itself (a folder, a spreadsheet and one document per video). It cannot see any other file in the owner's Google Drive, nor the owner's email, contacts or other Google data.

What it does with that access

Palantir writes the statistics of the owner's own Instagram, TikTok and YouTube posts into those files, so the owner can read them on his phone. It does not read the files back, and it does not share, sell or transfer any data to anyone.

Palantir's use and transfer of information received from Google APIs adheres to the Google API Services User Data Policy, including the Limited Use requirements.

Where data is kept

The statistics are kept in the owner's own database and in the owner's own Google Drive. The Google sign-in token is stored only on the owner's own computer or server.

Removing access

The owner can remove Palantir's access at any time from his Google account settings (Security, Third-party apps with account access), and can delete the files Palantir created like any other Drive file.
